Good coffee, made for the neighbourhood.

Northside Roast is a small corner coffee shop serving carefully roasted beans, simple food, and a warm room to sit in for a while. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.

A note on seating

We have a handful of tables and a long counter by the window. Seating is first come, first served, and we're always happy to pack your order to go.
