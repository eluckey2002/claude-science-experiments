Engineered for Bitcoin-native savings

Every feature is built around one principle: your bitcoin should compound in security as it compounds in value.

Automated Stacking

Schedule recurring buys down to the dollar or set dollar-cost-average rules that execute on-chain, so your position grows on autopilot — no manual orders, no missed weeks.

Multi-Sig Cold Storage

Keys are sharded across geographically distributed, air-gapped signers. No single point of failure, no custodial risk — your vault, your keys, mathematically enforced.

Every confirmation, every sat, streamed live from the chain to your dashboard. Full transaction history, cost-basis tracking, and exportable reports on demand.
