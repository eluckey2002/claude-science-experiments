The Protocol

Engineered for savers, not speculators.

Three primitives make Northside Vault the most disciplined way to hold Bitcoin for the long term.

True Self-Custody

Your keys are generated and encrypted on-device with a 3-of-5 multisig quorum. Northside never holds a signing key that can move your funds without you.

Automated Stacking

Schedule recurring buys straight to your vault address, sourced at spot with transparent, itemized network fees. Set it once, let the protocol compound.

On-Chain Transparency

Every transaction is independently verifiable on the Bitcoin ledger. Full audit trail, exportable proofs of reserve, zero black boxes.
