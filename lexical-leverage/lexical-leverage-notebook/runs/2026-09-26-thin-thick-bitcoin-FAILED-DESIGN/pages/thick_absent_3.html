Why Northside

Engineered for the paranoid.
Built for everyone.

Every design decision starts from a single question: what would a security engineer with no trust in third parties build for their own savings?

Non-Custodial by Design

Your keys never touch our servers. Vaults are secured with 2-of-3 multisig, split across your device, a hardware key, and an optional recovery guardian.

Automated Stacking

Set a recurring buy in seconds. Northside dollar-cost-averages into cold storage on your schedule, so you build a position without watching the chart.

On-Chain Transparency

Every reserve is independently verifiable. Publish a live proof-of-reserves attestation straight from the blockchain — no trust required, only math.
