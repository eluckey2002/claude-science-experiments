Why Northside Vault

Engineered For Trust

Every design decision optimizes for one thing: making sure your Bitcoin is verifiably, mathematically safe.

Non-Custodial by Design

Your keys, your Bitcoin. Northside Vault never takes custody — funds sit in a 2-of-3 multi-sig wallet with keys distributed across independent, audited signers.

Automated Stacking Engine

Set a recurring buy, round up everyday purchases to the nearest sat, or dollar-cost-average on a schedule. Accumulation runs on autopilot, rain or shine.

Real-Time Proof of Reserves

Every satoshi is verifiable on-chain. Our live reserves dashboard cryptographically proves that what's in the vault matches what's on your statement — no trust required.
