Why Northside

Engineered for security. Built for growth.

Every feature is designed around one principle: your bitcoin should be safer and more productive here than anywhere else.

Cold Storage Custody

Assets are secured in geographically distributed, multi-signature cold storage. Private keys never touch a hot server, and every withdrawal requires multi-party approval.

Automated DCA Vaults

Schedule recurring buys, round up everyday purchases, or set rules-based deposits. Northside Vault executes your strategy on-chain, every single time.

Real-Time Ledger Insights

Track cost basis, growth, and network fees in a live technical dashboard. Every satoshi is accounted for, down to the block.
